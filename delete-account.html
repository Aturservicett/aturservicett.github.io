Delete My Account

You have the right to delete your Aturservicett account and all associated personal data at any time. We process all deletion requests within 90 days.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to our support team using the address below.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address registered to your account.
  4. We will confirm receipt within 2 business days and complete the deletion within 90 days.
Send your request to: support@aturservicett.com

Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to your profile, reviews, and all account history. This action cannot be undone.

What gets deleted
  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile information (name, area, bio, photos)
  • Your reviews and ratings
  • Your service listings (if you are a tradesman)

Some data may be retained for up to 90 days in secure backups before being permanently overwritten. We retain records as required by Trinidad and Tobago law.

For more information about how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
