Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This is the plain-language version of our Privacy Policy. It aligns with the Trinidad and Tobago Data Protection Act 2011 and international transparency standards.

Aturservicett LLC runs the Aturservicett app. Questions? Email support@aturservicett.com.

1. What we collect

When you sign up: email address, first name, last name, and (optionally) your general area.

If you are a tradesman: trade details, service radius, rate, bio, phone number, and photos.

When you use the app: basic device and screen-visit data to keep the app working.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • Your precise GPS location.
  • The contents of your WhatsApp conversations.
  • Your contacts, calendar, or camera roll.
  • Payment or bank information (we do not process payments).

3. How we use your information

  • To register and log you in via email verification codes.
  • To let clients discover and contact tradesmen.
  • To display profiles, ratings, and reviews.
  • To send essential account notifications (never marketing).
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To comply with Trinidad and Tobago law.

4. Who sees your information

Other users see your first name and last initial. Your email is never shown publicly. Your phone number is shared only when a client taps the WhatsApp button.

5. Data security

We use HTTPS encryption and encrypted storage. We use passwordless login (email verification code only), so there is no password to be stolen or leaked.

6. Data retention

  • Active accounts: kept while your account exists.
  • Deleted accounts: personal data removed within 90 days.
  • Support emails: kept for up to 24 months after resolution.

7. Your rights

Under the Trinidad and Tobago Data Protection Act 2011, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Email support@aturservicett.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Clients must be at least 13 years old. Tradesmen must be at least 18. Contact support@aturservicett.com if you believe your child provided data without permission.

9. International data transfer

Our providers (Resend, Supabase, Google, WhatsApp, Apple) may store data outside Trinidad and Tobago. By using Aturservicett, you agree to this transfer.

10. Policy updates

We will notify you through the app or by email at least 14 days before important changes take effect.
